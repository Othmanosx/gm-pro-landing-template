# Blog roadmap

One post every two days, live at 08:00 UTC on its date once the 08:15 deploy runs. Dates are real release dates: if this merges after a date below, move every unreleased date forward instead of shipping a post "in the past". Sources for each row are in the 2026-10-25 research pack summarized in `docs/blog-ops.md`.

Before adding a row: no title may share 60%+ of its content words with an existing one (`pnpm blog:check` enforces it for written posts). Fewer, deeper posts beat one post per keyword variation.
